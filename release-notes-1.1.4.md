## MediaBridge Radio 1.1.4

- **Plays and pauses with the radio:** your music plays while the car radio or the garage jukebox is on and tuned to MediaBridge, and pauses when both are off, when you tune to another station, or when you pause the game. Turning off one while the other is still on keeps the music going.
- **Skips only while looking at the radio:** in the car, the channel buttons (D-pad left/right) only skip songs while you're looking at the radio, like the normal stations.
- **Fixed a crash** while the game starts or loads a save (seen on Proton stable and Windows).

Tested on Windows and Steam Deck (Proton stable, 10.0, 11.0-2, Experimental and GE-Proton9-26).

**Install:** unzip and follow `README.txt`. Needs [UE4SS (experimental)](https://github.com/UE4SS-RE/RE-UE4SS/releases/tag/experimental-latest) and [MediaBridge](https://github.com/AlexUnknown37/MediaBridge/releases).
