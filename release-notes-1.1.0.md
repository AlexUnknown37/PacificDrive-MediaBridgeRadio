## MediaBridge Radio 1.1.0

Play your own music on the Pacific Drive car radio. The radio's channel buttons skip songs, and the radio shows "Song Title - Artist".

**New in 1.1.0**
- **Steam Deck / Linux support** (with the Linux version of MediaBridge). Tested on a Steam Deck.
- Fixed skipping two songs per press on Steam Deck.

**1.0.0**
- First release: skip songs with the radio buttons (keybinds and on-screen buttons), show the song name on the radio.

**Download:** `MediaBridgeRadio-1.1.0.zip`. It has the mod folder, the ready-made radio playlist and `README.txt` with install steps for Windows and Steam Deck.

**Needs**
- Pacific Drive v1.7.1 or newer
- UE4SS experimental: https://github.com/UE4SS-RE/RE-UE4SS/releases/tag/experimental-latest
- MediaBridge: https://github.com/AlexUnknown37/MediaBridge/releases
